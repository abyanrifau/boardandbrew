02 | The Opportunity

A prime spot on Thulusdhoo’s busiest path

The cafe sits directly in front of the bridge to Cokes, one of the most famous surf breaks in the Maldives. Every surfer, day-tripper and guest heading to the point walks right past our front.

  • Prime location

    Front-row position on the main route to the Cokes surf point.

  • Tourism is booming

    Foreign and local tourism is growing fast and reaching new levels.

  • Thulusdhoo is next

    The island is set to reach its highest visitor numbers yet.

03 | The Rebrand

From Peace and Joy to Board & Brew

A new name and identity built around what makes this spot special: the surf, the drinks and the view. Natural thatch, pale timber and soft linen replace the red umbrellas and plastic furniture, giving the cafe a calm, modern beach feel that stands out from the path.

  • Surf-inspired
  • Laid-back
  • Modern
  • Trendy
